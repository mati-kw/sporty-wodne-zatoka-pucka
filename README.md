# Uwagi do planu ochrony Zatoki Puckiej

The Maritime Office in Gdynia has put the draft protection plan for Natura 2000 **Zatoka Pucka (PLB220005)** out for public comments. The draft, dated 22 October 2026, would close or narrow parts of the bay that people use for kitesurfing, windsurfing and similar sports: Ryf Mew and a 500 m belt, a planned reserve on that shoal, a stretch of the Hel shore, a 200 m strip along the beach, a recreational-pressure counter, and the rules for regattas.

Comments are accepted until **13 November 2026**. A signed PDF goes to Narodowa Fundacja Ochrony Środowiska at **zarzad@nfos.org.pl**, with **Zatoka Pucka** in the subject.

## Aim

Help one person write their own comments and leave with a PDF they can sign and send.

The form asks about the annex points that affect people on the water. Each answer continues a sentence aimed at that point, so the letter stays tied to the draft. Four short examples sit under every question. On a new visit each question draws a different example, which the visitor can switch, edit, or replace. The whole letter stays visible and editable, and that text is the PDF.

Nothing is sent to a server. The draft stays in the browser. Signing happens on gov.pl with a trusted profile (profil zaufany), which mObywatel can confirm in under a minute. A qualified signature is not required. The announcement also allows a scan of a letter signed by hand.
